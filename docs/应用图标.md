# 应用图标

图标使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，主题为“相机与无线素材同步”。图案采用蓝色圆角底、浅色相机和无线信号符号，外围透明，不含文字。

资源保存在 `Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`，包含 macOS 的 16、32、128、256、512 点及其 2 倍屏幕倍率。1024 像素源图为 `icon_512x512@2x.png`，其他尺寸通过系统 sips 工具缩放生成。

Xcode 工程生成脚本将此资源目录加入构建，并设置 `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME = AppIcon`，构建时自动生成应用图标及对应的 Info.plist 配置。

应用启动时还会直接读取打包的 `AppIcon.icns` 并设置 Dock 图标，避免旧版本图标缓存影响运行中的应用。更新后需要完全退出旧进程，再启动新构建；此修正已进行编译和资源校验，Dock 实际显示由用户自行验证。

## 实际生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS application icon for 相机素材同步 (WiFiSync), a utility that transfers photos and videos wirelessly from an action camera to a Mac. Primary request: one beautifully crafted, immediately legible native Mac app icon combining a compact camera silhouette with subtle wireless/sync symbolism. Style: refined dimensional macOS icon, simple clean geometry, crisp edges, polished soft depth, deep navy rounded square tile with luminous blue and turquoise accents and a pale camera body. A single large centered camera lens is the focal point, with a small clean wireless arc motif integrated above the camera. Composition: square 1024 x 1024 image; rounded square tile with generous transparent margin around it, all corners fully visible; generous negative space, recognizable at 16 px. No lettering, no words, no logo of existing brands, no watermark, no multiple variants, no mockup or surrounding desktop. Outside the rounded square icon tile must be transparent.
```
